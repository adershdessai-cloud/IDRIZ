## 2. Engineering Excellence

### Brand & Visual System

The site follows the official IDRIZ brand system with strict consistency:

- **Royal Blue** `#004A99` — primary actions, accents, and trust signals  
- **Charcoal** `#2C2C2C` — text and premium dark sections  
- **Gold** `#FFD700` — highlights, ratings, and key brand emphasis (including “IDRIZ Quality” in the hero)

The header uses a refined **glass-style** look that becomes solid white on scroll. Premium gradients, clean spacing, and consistent typography (**Inter** for body text and **Montserrat** for headings) create a polished, executive-ready presence.

### Micro-interactions

Subtle motion is used to feel premium—not distracting:

- Sections **fade in and slide up** as visitors scroll  
- The **Get a Quote** button includes a gentle pulse (and pauses on hover)  
- A custom **blue track / gold thumb** scrollbar reinforces brand identity  
- Navigation links show a blue underline that grows from the left on hover  

### Responsive Design

Layouts were tuned for real devices and screens:

| Breakpoint | Experience |
|------------|------------|
| **320px** (small phones) | Single-column layout, clear hero text, hamburger navigation |
| **768px** (tablets) | Balanced two-column sections where appropriate |
| **1024px** (laptops) | Full desktop structure and spacing |
| **1440px** (large desktops) | Comfortable wide layout with refined hierarchy |

## 3. Performance & SEO Audit Results

The build targets a **95+ Lighthouse** performance and quality profile, with launch-minded optimizations throughout.

### Speed & loading

- Fonts load in a **non-blocking** way so content appears quickly  
- Images below the fold use **lazy loading**  
- The hero image loads immediately to protect first-impression speed (LCP)  
- Early connections (`preconnect`) are used for fonts and key external assets  

### Discoverability & sharing

- **LocalBusiness** structured data for Dubai-focused search understanding  
- **Open Graph** and **Twitter** meta tags for clean social sharing previews  
- Keyword-aligned titles and descriptions for manpower supply and facilities management in Dubai  

### Mobile conversion details

- **Click-to-call** phone links (`tel:`)  
- **One-tap email** links (`mailto:`)  
- Clear contact details and a quote form designed for mobile use  

---

## 4. Accessibility & Compliance

The website is built toward **WCAG 2.1 AA** expectations, including:

- A **Skip to main content** link for keyboard users  
- Clear **ARIA labels** on icon-only and interactive controls  
- Visible **focus states** for keyboard navigation  
- A **high-contrast** brand palette for readable text on light and dark sections  
- Proper labels on all form fields  

This supports inclusivity, professionalism, and a smoother experience for every visitor.

## 5. Quality Assurance (QA) Pass

A rigorous QA pass was completed before handoff, including automated and manual checks for:

- Clean page load behavior (no script/console failures in core interactions)  
- Social links opening in a **new tab** with secure `target="_blank"` attributes  
- Contact form validation with success messaging and **no page reload**  
- Correct **alt text** on the logo and all key images  
- Working phone and email links in the contact section  

The site passed final QA and is cleared for go-live.
